## Problem

imageCLI exhibits the strongest N×M cascade pressure of the 3-CLI worker fleet (per lyra #1277 framework, cross-repo audit 2026-05-20). N=9 engines (growing); M=7+ concerns per 2-phase engine file. The hottest zone is the 3-engine quantization family (`flux2-klein` / `flux2-klein-fp8` / `flux2-klein-fp4`): **75% file similarity**, **76 lines word-for-word identical** across all 3 files, **5 duplicated methods × 3 files** (`encode_and_generate`, `generate_from_embeddings`, `encode_prompt`, `load_for_encode`, `start_generation_phase`). Recent refactors (`engine` sub-package split, `helpers.py`, `_pulid/`, `nvfp4/`, `validators.py`) show the team fighting the pattern one file at a time without naming the root cause.

Two additional active anti-patterns: (1) socket-bound daemon leaks `str(exc)` to clients on 3 paths in `daemon_handlers.py` (NATS path correctly typed via `WorkerError`, socket path is the unsanitized fallback); (2) NATS subject literals hardcoded at `nats/adapter.py:23-24` while llmCLI imports `llm.SUBJECTS` from `roxabi-contracts` as reference pattern; PuLID engines duplicate InsightFace + EVA-CLIP loading verbatim at 3 sites.

Why now: the upstream contracts work (lyra #1291, closed 2026-05) has landed on `staging` — `image.SUBJECTS` and `sanitize_for_wire` are ready to consume. A 4th quant variant (anticipated quanto int8 for Ampere) would otherwise copy the 76-line block a 4th time.

## Premise Validity

**Success in 6 months:** Adding a 4th quant variant (e.g. quanto int8 for Ampere) or a new PuLID engine costs ≤30 LOC subclass — no copy of the 76-line 2-phase block, no copy of the 7-line InsightFace block. Zero `str(exc)` on socket paths. NATS subjects sourced from `roxabi_contracts.image.SUBJECTS`. `roxabi-nats` aligned to `branch=staging` (no version drift across the 3-CLI fleet); upstream `wait_ready` / contract bumps propagate via `uv sync`.

**Failure in 6 months:** A 4th quant variant ships with another verbatim copy of `encode_and_generate` + `generate_from_embeddings`, OR a daemon error leaks a file path / stack trace over the socket again, OR `roxabi-nats` releases v0.5 with a contract change and imageCLI silently misses it because of the tag pin. Falsifiable conditions, ¬vibes:

- `grep -c "def encode_and_generate" src/imagecli/engines/*.py` > 1 (target: exactly 1, in the base class).
- `grep -F "str(exc)" src/imagecli/daemon_handlers.py | wc -l` > 0 (target: 0).
- `grep -E '^\s*roxabi-nats.*tag\s*=' pyproject.toml | wc -l` > 0 (target: 0; must use `branch=`).

**Simplest alternative:** Just do the 4 cross-repo follow-ups — switch pin to `branch=staging`, declare `roxabi-contracts` direct dep, swap 1 `SUBJECT` import, swap 3 `str(exc)` → `sanitize_for_wire`. Skip `TwoPhaseBase`, `PuLIDMixin`, `UnknownEngineError`, `_check_nvfp4_requirements` extraction. Total: ~6 small edits.

**Why not simplest:** Cross-repo-only addresses 4 small leaks but leaves the hottest N×M zone untouched (75% similarity in flux2-klein trio; 76 LOC verbatim × 3 files). The next quant variant (quanto int8 for Ampere is already anticipated; LoRA + NVFP4 is another) would copy 76 lines a 4th time, plus another 7 lines of InsightFace boilerplate if it's a PuLID composition. Per audit, `TwoPhaseBase` extraction alone eliminates ~150 LOC and prevents the 4th copy. The cross-repo work + `TwoPhaseBase` + `PuLIDMixin` together is the **bounded consolidation** the audit recommends; pure cross-repo is the cheap-but-shallow option that locks in the local cascade.
